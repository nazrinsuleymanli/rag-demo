Trendyol Frequently Asked Questions

DELIVERY

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 1 to 3 business days for most products. Some products shipped from international sellers may take 5 to 10 business days.

How can I track my order?
You can track your order from the "My Orders" section in the app or website. Once your order ships, you receive a tracking number by SMS and email.

Is there a delivery fee?
Orders above 200 TL qualify for free shipping. For orders below 200 TL, a delivery fee of 19.99 TL applies.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
You can change the delivery address only before the order has been shipped. Go to "My Orders", select the order, and tap "Change Address".

RETURNS AND REFUNDS

What is the return policy?
You can return most products within 15 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging.

How do I start a return?
Go to "My Orders", select the product, tap "Return", choose a reason, and select a cargo drop-off point. Returns are free of charge.

How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 2 to 10 business days after the returned item reaches the seller. The money is returned to your original payment method.

Which products cannot be returned?
Underwear, swimwear, cosmetics that have been opened, and personalized products cannot be returned for hygiene and customization reasons.

ORDERS AND CANCELLATION

Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel an order for free before it has been shipped. After shipping, you need to use the return process instead.

Can I exchange a product for a different size?
Trendyol does not offer direct size exchange. You should return the product and place a new order for the correct size.

PAYMENT

What payment methods are accepted?
Trendyol accepts credit cards, debit cards, and the Trendyol Wallet. Some sellers also support pay-at-door with cash or card.

What is Trendyol Wallet?
Trendyol Wallet is a digital balance where refunds and promotional rewards are stored. You can use it to pay for future orders.

Is it safe to pay online?
All payments are secured with 3D Secure verification. Trendyol does not store your full card number.

ACCOUNT AND SUPPORT

How do I contact customer service?
You can reach customer service through the live chat in the app, or through the Help Center on the website, 24 hours a day.

How do I change my password?
Go to "Account" then "Account Information" then "Change Password". You will need your current password to set a new one.
